Affiliate Disclosure

How affiliate links work on this website and what that means for you.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Prime Perform Review is a reader-supported, independently operated informational website. This page explains what affiliate links are, how they appear on this site, and why they exist.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a special URL that tells a website you arrived there from our site. If you make a purchase after following that link, the merchant may pay us a small commission for referring you. The price you pay is not increased because you used our link.

Which links on this site are affiliate links?

Some links to the official Prime Perform offer page and to external checkout pages are affiliate links. They are the “Learn More,” “Visit Prime Perform,” and offer-card buttons you see throughout the page. Non-affiliate links (e.g. internal page anchors, or links in the FAQ explaining general product facts) are not affiliate links.

Does this affect what you pay?

No. A commission never increases your purchase price. The commission is paid out of the merchant’s revenue.

Does this affect what we write?

No. Our editorial content is independent. We do not publish favorable content in exchange for commission, and we do not receive payment from the seller to write reviews. Our goal is to present verified, useful information and let you decide.

What we are not

  • We are not the manufacturer or seller of Prime Perform.
  • We are not an authorized retailer or reseller.
  • We do not ship, fulfill, or handle customer-service inquiries for the product itself.

Third-party website responsibilities

When you click an affiliate link and land on an external website, that site’s own terms, privacy policy, refund policy, and checkout terms apply. We recommend reviewing those before completing a purchase.

Federal Trade Commission compliance

This disclosure is provided in accordance with the Federal Trade Commission’s guidelines concerning use of endorsements and testimonials in advertising. The affiliate relationships described here are disclosed to you so you can make an informed decision when you choose to click our links.

Site ownership

Prime Perform Review is an independent site. It is not owned or operated by the makers of Prime Perform and is not affiliated with the manufacturer or its parent company.

Changes
